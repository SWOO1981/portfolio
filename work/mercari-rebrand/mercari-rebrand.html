Mercari Rebrand: Aligning Executive Vision, Design Systems, and Team Development

A UX Management Case Study

UX Design Manager

Brand Identity + Design Systems

Eight weeks

Overview

At Mercari, I led a CEO-driven brand initiative that carried a pivotal marketing campaign, unblocked a stalled design token initiative, and created growth opportunities for a Senior Visual Designer who joined my team following a recent restructuring.

The Challenge

Conflicting stakeholder expectations, CEO-mandated assets, and fractured creative resourcing threatened alignment and follow-through. Owning the project meant clarifying scope and generating consensus on strategy to deliver a cohesive brand direction within an ambitious eight-week timeline.

What I Did

  • Persuaded executives to take a “brand evolution” approach rather than attempt a risky full-scale rebrand in eight weeks.
  • Grounded creative direction in pragmatic implementation to ensure a smooth rollout.
  • Leveraged the rollout to unlock design token adoption which had been blocked for two years.
  • Furthered my Senior Visual Designer’s professional development through visible brand stewardship.

Where We Landed

  1. A fresh take on our brand identity with production-ready assets in eight weeks.
  2. Organizational commitment for design tokens validated through dark mode theming.
  3. Increased Senior Designer’s visibility through ownership of brand guidelines and review process.
